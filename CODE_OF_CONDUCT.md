# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want LZY to be a place where someone writing their first program and
someone who has written compilers for twenty years can both ask a question
without worrying about how it will be received.

We pledge to make taking part in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

- Being kind, and assuming the other person is acting in good faith
- Taking a disagreement about code as a disagreement about code
- Giving criticism that is specific, and about the work
- Accepting criticism gracefully, and saying so when you were wrong
- Explaining things without condescension, especially to beginners
- Asking "what am I missing?" before "you are wrong"

Behaviour that does not:

- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission
- Dismissing a question as stupid, or a person as not worth answering
- Anything else that would be unprofessional in a workplace

**A note on beginners.** LZY exists so that people can learn to program. A
question that seems obvious to you may be the hardest thing someone has
attempted this year. Answer it, or say nothing.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces — the repository, issues,
pull requests, discussions and releases — and whenever someone is
representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the project maintainers privately. The
simplest route is GitHub's private vulnerability reporting on this repository
(**Security -> Report a vulnerability**), which only maintainers can see, or
contact a maintainer directly.

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. We will
respect the privacy and security of whoever reports an incident.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith
may face temporary or permanent consequences as decided by the other
maintainers.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers will follow these steps in deciding what to do:

**1. Correction.** *Impact: language or behaviour judged unprofessional or
unwelcome.* A private, written warning explaining what was wrong and why. A
public apology may be requested.

**2. Warning.** *Impact: a single incident, or a series of them.* A warning,
with consequences for continued behaviour. No interaction with the people
involved, including unsolicited contact with those enforcing this Code, for
a set period. Breaking these terms may lead to a ban.

**3. Temporary ban.** *Impact: a serious violation, or sustained
inappropriate behaviour.* A temporary ban from any interaction with the
project. No public or private interaction with the people involved during
this period. Breaking these terms may lead to a permanent ban.

**4. Permanent ban.** *Impact: a pattern of violation, harassment of an
individual, or aggression toward a group.* A permanent ban from any public
interaction with the project.
